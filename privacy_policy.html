Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

SafeUnfollow ("the App", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

1. We Don't Collect Your Personal Data

SafeUnfollow operates entirely on your device. We do not:

  • Collect, store, or transmit your Instagram data
  • Require you to log in with any account
  • Upload your follower/following information to any server
  • Share any data with third parties
  • Track your activity or behavior

The Instagram data ZIP file you select is processed exclusively on your device. Your data never leaves your phone.

2. Information We Do Collect

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect limited, anonymized data:

  • Google AdMob — Serves advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID) for ad personalization. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings under Google → Ads.
  • Google Firebase Analytics — Collects anonymized usage statistics (e.g., number of ZIP files opened, app version). No personally identifiable information is collected.

3. Advertising ID

The App requests the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission solely for Google AdMob to serve ads. This permission is used in accordance with Google Play's Ads policy.

4. Data Storage

All app preferences (such as your theme choice) are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. No data is synced to the cloud.

5. Your Rights

Since SafeUnfollow does not collect or store your personal data on any server, there is no account to manage and no data to request or delete from us. Your Instagram data that you load into the app remains exclusively on your device and is cleared when you delete the analysis result or uninstall the app.

6. Children's Privacy

SafeUnfollow is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
